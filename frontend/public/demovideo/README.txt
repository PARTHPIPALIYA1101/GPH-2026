Demo Videos Directory (/demovideo)

Place your demo video and audio files here:
- v2.mp4 (Primary demo video stream)
- v1.mp4 (Alternative demo video stream)
- v1.mp3 (Demo audio / dispatch stream)

These files will be served automatically by the web server at:
  /demovideo/v2.mp4
  /demovideo/v1.mp4
  /demovideo/v1.mp3

You can also use the "CHOOSE FROM STORAGE" button on the /cameras page to load any video or audio file directly from anywhere on your computer disk!
